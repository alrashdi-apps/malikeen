Privacy Policy — Malikeen

Last updated: November 2026

Introduction

Malikeen is for property owners and property managers. We respect your privacy and design the app to work locally on your iPhone without collecting data on our servers.

The Data You Enter

Your iPhone may store: names of buildings and units, tenants’ names, mobile numbers, ID number (optional), contract and rent details, payments and expenses.

Where Data Is Stored

Your data is saved on your iPhone. Syncing through your Apple Account’s iCloud starts when you turn on “iCloud sync” in the app’s settings. It’s then saved in your account on Apple’s servers, which may be outside your country. We don’t send your data to our servers, and we don’t sell it or share it for advertising.

Backup and Sharing

You can save a backup or export a spreadsheet from Settings through other apps such as Files or Mail. You control this sharing, and the backup file isn’t encrypted.

External Links

The app may open WhatsApp or the Phone app to contact tenants. These actions happen outside the app and under those services’ policies.

Deleting Data

You can delete all data in Settings › Data Management, and the financial journal stays on your iPhone as a record of what happened. You can delete the app to remove all data from your iPhone, including the journal.

Contact

Questions: alrashdi.apps@gmail.com

If you send a problem report, we receive your email and what’s in the report, and we use them only to fix the problem.

The app is a tool for organising your work and doesn’t give legal advice. Following contracts and regulations is the user’s responsibility.
