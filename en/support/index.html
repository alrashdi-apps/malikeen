If sync fails

  1. Check your iCloud storage in Settings › your name › iCloud. If it’s full, free up space or add more.
  2. Use the same Apple Account on all your devices, and make sure iCloud is on for Malikeen.
  3. Check your internet connection.
  4. Check the status of Apple’s services on the System Status page.
  5. Update iOS and Malikeen to the latest version.
  6. If the error continues, open Settings › Sync › Sync Log › Share Log, and send it to alrashdi.apps@gmail.com.
